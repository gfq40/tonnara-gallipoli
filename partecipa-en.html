Collective Memory & Community

Building Memory Together

The history of the Gallipoli Tuna Fishery is not only in books; it lives in our homes, in grandparents' memories and in the stories of those who lived at sea.

Why your contribution matters

Every photograph is a piece of history resurfacing

We are building the digital archive and shaping the path of the future Gallipoli Tuna Fishery Multimedia Museum. We want this museum to be the collective story of an entire community.

Whether it is an old yellowed photo, a ledger, a letter, a worn fishing tool or an oral memory passed down through the family, your material will make a difference.

Open Archive

For Researchers and Scholars

The museum offers scholars on-site consultation of the paper archive and an open, machine-readable digital catalogue.

Paper archive: Red Books (2 volumes), "Municipal Tuna Fishery" files and documents available for on-site consultation by appointment.

Digital catalogues: the catalogue of Piri Reis charts (Kitab-ı Bahriye, W.658, CC0) and digitized materials is available in open format.

Citations: for the use of sources and images, follow the instructions on the How to Cite page.

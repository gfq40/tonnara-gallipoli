Ancestral memories

Pieces of history passed down through generations

The most important memories are not just names: they are the gestures, objects and stories passed down by families. This page is dedicated to those who preserve the tuna fishery’s intangible heritage.

  • Grandfather’s hook: a simple object that tells a lifetime at sea, passed down with the advice to always stay calm when working with the nets.
  • The sweet-and-sour tuna recipe: a flavor that binds generations, kept by the women who cared for tables and celebrations after the slaughter.
  • The pre-dawn song: work songs that accompanied the preparation of the nets and the departure to sea, preserved in grandparents’ voices and now part of the museum’s identity.

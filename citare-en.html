Guidelines and Permalinks

How to Cite

Citation formats and stable permalinks for the website, the charts, the photographs and the documents: every object of the heritage can be cited unambiguously.

The website

Recommended citation

Author-date format (Chicago / APA style)

Multimedia Museum of the Gallipoli Tonnara. "Historical Timeline." Gallipoli. Accessed 4 August 2026. https://www.tonnaragallipoli.it/cronologia-en.html.

Note format (Chicago style)

1. Multimedia Museum of the Gallipoli Tonnara, "Historical Timeline", accessed 4 August 2026, https://www.tonnaragallipoli.it/cronologia-en.html.

Always include the date of access, as digital content may be updated.

Stable permalinks

Citable objects

Charts by Piri Reis (Kitab-ı Bahriye, W.658)

The 10 charts of the Gulf of Taranto can be cited individually from the Archive. Reference citation:

Piri Reis, Kitab-ı Bahriye, Walters Art Museum, ms. W.658, f. 206b (CC0). "The Coast from Lecce to Gallipoli", in Multimedia Museum of the Gallipoli Tonnara, Archive & Gallery — Historical Cartography. Accessed 4 August 2026. https://www.tonnaragallipoli.it/archivio-en.html.

Period photographs (1904–1909)

Photographs of the fishing and of the mattanza (including the shots of Eugenio Interguglielmi, 1904), public domain, museum archive.

Interguglielmi, E., The Tuna Fishing (1904), photograph, public domain. In Multimedia Museum of the Gallipoli Tonnara, Archive & Gallery. Accessed 4 August 2026. https://www.tonnaragallipoli.it/archivio-en.html.

Digitised documents (PDF)

The 1833 record and the ancient writings are downloadable from the Archive.

Gallipoli. Ancient writings archive (1833), PDF. In Multimedia Museum of the Gallipoli Tonnara, Archive. Accessed 4 August 2026. https://www.tonnaragallipoli.it/pdf/Gallipoli_Archivio_delle_scritture_antic.pdf.

Glossary entries

Every entry has a dedicated permalink (e.g. glossario-en.html#rais).

Multimedia Museum of the Gallipoli Tonnara, "Rais", Scholarly Glossary. Accessed 4 August 2026. https://www.tonnaragallipoli.it/glossario-en.html#rais.
Use and rights

Notes on use

The charts by Piri Reis (W.658) and the period photographs are in the public domain and reusable, with source attribution. The digitised PDF documents are published by the museum for study purposes.

The non-digitised materials (Red Book I and II, "Tonnara Comunale" fascicles) are available for consultation on site upon request: use the contact form for researchers.
